Lightning

The most an outgoing payment may cost to route. It is a ceiling, not a charge: the real fee is usually a few sats, and only that is deducted. The minimum is what small payments and zaps depend on — most nodes charge about a sat per hop whatever the amount — so set too low, they fail with "no route". The limit is set aside from the identity's balance while a payment is in flight, and it counts toward the per-payment cap.
